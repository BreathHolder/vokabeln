What this app includes

  • 🃏 Dual modes

    Flashcard Mode: See the English prompt, then reveal the German spelling, phonetic guide, and spelling reminders for capitalization, double consonants, and umlauts.

    Spelling Quiz: Type the German phrase using the on-screen umlaut keys (ä, ö, ü, ß).

  • 🔊 Audio pronunciation

    Hear each phrase with your browser’s German (de-DE) text-to-speech voice at a learner-friendly pace.

  • ✍️ Smart spelling evaluation

    Get distinct feedback for spelling mistakes and near-matches that only need capitalization or punctuation corrections.

  • 👍 Study deck controls

    I know this! removes the current card for the rest of the browser session, helping you focus on words that still need practice.

    Show all cards restores every hidden card, while Shuffle cards randomizes the remaining deck for a fresh practice order.
